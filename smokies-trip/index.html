Accessibility and pace

  • Walks between zones are planned at 1.5–2 times normal: 20–30 min across the park, 25–30 min on climbs.
  • Steepest stretches: Timber Canyon up to Wildwood Grove, Craftsman's Valley, and the drop into Jukebox Junction. Use the Dollywood app's accessible routes.
  • Dollywood rents single-rider scooters and wheelchairs daily; some scooters can be reserved online at least 48 hours ahead.
  • The Ride Accessibility Center (next to the Emporium) issues Boarding Passes for the holder and up to 5 companions. It is not a skip-the-line pass.
  • Theater wheelchair seating is first come, first served; arrive 15–20 min early.
  • Companion care restrooms: Showstreet, Country Fair, Craftsman's Valley, Owens Farm, Village First Aid, Wilderness Pass.
  • Trams run only between parking and the gate; the train is a round-trip ride, not transport.
