# Pistol Squat Resources

## Knowledge

- [ExRx.net: Single Leg Squat (pistol)](https://exrx.net/WeightExercises/Quadriceps/BWSingleLegSquat)
  **Primary form source.** Free leg extended forward off the floor, knee tracks the foot, squat as low as possible, stand to a straight knee. Lists easier versions (bar self-assisted, box) and counterweighted (dumbbell) versions. Use for: rep standard, regressions.
- [GMB Fitness: Pistol Squat Progression (bottom-up method)](https://gmb.io/pistol/)
  Names ankle dorsiflexion as the most common limiter; modified-lunge knee pulses and deep-squat work as drills; argues the bottom position is the hardest part. Use for: ankle drill, deep-squat prerequisite, bottom strength.
- [r/bodyweightfitness: Thoughts on the leg progression of the recommended routine](https://www.reddit.com/r/bodyweightfitness/comments/52jzf1/thoughts_on_the_leg_progression_of_the/)
  Practitioner consensus: chair pistols, then box pistols at decreasing height, then counterweighted pistols; supported pistols using less and less help. Use for: ladder order.
- [r/bodyweightfitness: Pistol Squats, what's worked for you?](https://www.reddit.com/r/bodyweightfitness/comments/369a8m/pistol_squats_whats_worked_for_you/)
  Counterweight held forward helps balance and ankle limits; bottom-position holds. Use for: counterweight rung.
- [Tsatsouline, "Grease the Groove for Strength" (reprint)](https://humanmachine.wordpress.com/2007/06/22/grease-the-groove-for-strength/)
  Frequent, fresh, never-to-failure practice. Pavel also teaches the pistol in *The Naked Warrior*. Use for: frequency rules.
