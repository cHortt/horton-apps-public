Glossary

Pistol squat
Full-depth single-leg squat with the free leg held straight out in front.
Assisted pistol
Pistol while holding a support (the tower upright), using as little help as possible.
Box pistol
Single-leg sit to a box or bench, pause, stand; box height drops over time.
Counterweight
Light dumbbell held forward at arm's length to shift balance forward.
Ankle dorsiflexion
Bending the ankle so the knee travels forward over the toes with the heel down.
Rung / level
A step on the progression ladder (1–6). Pass = 3 clean reps per leg on 2 days.
Grease the groove
Frequent, fresh, never-to-failure practice.
Knee-to-wall test
Weight-bearing lunge test: farthest toe-to-wall distance at which the knee touches the wall with the heel down.
Heel lift
Temporary board or plate under the heel that reduces the ankle bend a squat needs.
