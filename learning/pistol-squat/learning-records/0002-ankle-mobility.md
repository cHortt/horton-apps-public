# Ankles are a primary limiter: measure and train them directly

On Oct 15 2026 Collin confirmed he can't sit in a narrow, heels-down deep squat, matching the big forward lean in his assisted pistols. Ankle dorsiflexion is the most common pistol limiter (GMB), so it gets its own track: a weekly knee-to-wall test (rough target ~4 in / 10 cm; ~¾ in change is real), daily knee-over-toe pulses, straight- and bent-knee calf stretches, and a supported deep squat hold.

**Implications**: Pistol practice may use a ~1 in heel lift for now, removed gradually as knee-to-wall improves. Knee-to-wall is logged as its own type and re-measured on the Oct 28 test day. If he reports a front-ankle pinch rather than a calf stretch, adjust toward joint-focused work (band) rather than more stretching.
