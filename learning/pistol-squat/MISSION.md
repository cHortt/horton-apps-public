# Mission: 3 clean, unassisted pistol squats on each leg

## Why
Personal goal: Collin wants single-leg strength, mobility, and balance to match his upper-body goals (20 pull-ups, 20 dips), trained the same short-session way.

## Success looks like
- 3 clean pistol squats in a row on each leg: no hand support, free heel stays down, controlled descent to the bottom, stand to a straight knee, free foot off the floor
- Can self-assess which rung of the ladder he is on and when to move up
- Practice fits into existing quick tower visits

## Constraints
- Equipment: pull-up tower upright (for assistance), a bench/box/chair plus stackable items for height, light dumbbells
- Time: many short sessions (minutes, not hours)
- No deadline; consistency over speed
- Joints first: any knee pain means back off a rung

## Out of scope
- Weighted pistols, jumping pistols, high-rep leg endurance
