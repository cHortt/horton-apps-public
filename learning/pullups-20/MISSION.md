# Mission: 20 strict pull-ups, non-stop

## Why
Personal goal: Collin wants to do 20 strict, dead-hang pull-ups in one unbroken set, while training around a full life on a busy schedule.

## Success looks like
- 20 strict reps: dead hang at the bottom, chin over bar at the top, no kipping, no rest off the bar
- A training routine made of short sessions that fits into ordinary days at home
- Knows how to adjust the plan himself when he stalls

## Constraints
- Equipment: home pull-up bar, dumbbells
- Time: many short sessions (minutes, not hours); no long workouts
- No deadline; consistency over speed
- Joints first: elbow or shoulder pain means back off

## Out of scope
- Weighted pull-up strength, muscle-ups, kipping/CrossFit pull-ups, physique goals
