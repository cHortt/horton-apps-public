Glossary

The words this course uses. Terms get added as you put them to use.

Strict pull-up: One rep that goes from a dead hang to your chin over the bar, with no kip or swing. Avoid: "a pull-up" for any rep that's swung.

Max: The most strict pull-ups you can do in one unbroken set when you're fresh. Your current max is 8. Avoid: PR, AMRAP

Grease the groove (GTG): Practising a movement in frequent sub-maximal sets spread through the day, always stopping well before failure. Avoid: microdosing

Groove set: One GTG set of about half your max. In this course, "set" means a groove set unless it says otherwise.

Failure: The point where you can't finish another strict rep. You stay away from it except on test days.

Test: A single max set done fresh after a short warm-up. It sets the size of your next groove set.

Training set (coming in Lesson 2): Armstrong's fixed rep count, sized so you can finish 9 sets with 60 seconds of rest between them.
