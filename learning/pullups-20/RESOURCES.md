# 20 Pull-ups Resources

## Knowledge

- [PDF: "The Armstrong Pull-up Program" (Maj. Charles Lewis Armstrong, hosted by USMC Officer Selection)](https://officer.marines.com/docs/oso/physical_training/The_Armstrong_Workout.pdf)
  **Primary source.** Five-day weekly pull-up program built explicitly for reaching a 20-rep set; defines "training sets", pyramid day, max-effort day. Use for: weeks 3+ structure, training-set sizing.
- [Article: "Grease the Groove for Strength" by Pavel Tsatsouline (reprint of the MILO article, with Clarence Bass's experiment)](https://humanmachine.wordpress.com/2007/06/22/grease-the-groove-for-strength/)
  Origin of grease-the-groove (GTG): frequent, sub-maximal, never-to-failure practice; includes a 60-year-old reaching 20 chins. Use for: GTG rules (stay fresh, avoid failure, one slow negative max).
- [StrongFirst: Grease-the-Groove article archive](https://www.strongfirst.com/tag/grease-the-groove/)
  Pavel's organisation's collection of GTG articles. Use for: deeper reading on frequency and staying fresh.
- [Q&A: "How can I reconcile the need for rest with greasing the groove on pull ups?" (Fitness Stack Exchange)](https://fitness.stackexchange.com/questions/3448/how-can-i-reconcile-the-need-for-rest-with-greasing-the-groove-on-pull-ups)
  Explains why low-rep frequent sets need little recovery. Use for: recovery questions.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/bodyweightfitness: "My Long Journey to 20 Pullups"](https://www.reddit.com/r/bodyweightfitness/comments/dcsf9p/my_long_journey_to_20_pullups/)
  Real trainee log: GTG alone plateaued for him; Armstrong, EMOMs, pyramids and regular max tests broke it. Use for: plateau troubleshooting; reason we add one test/density day.
- [r/bodyweightfitness](https://www.reddit.com/r/bodyweightfitness/)
  Large, well-moderated bodyweight community. Use for: form checks (post a video), program critique.

## Gaps
- No peer-reviewed study specifically on GTG for pull-up max reps found yet. Claims about it are coach/practitioner evidence.
- Elbow/shoulder overuse guidance from a clinician source still needed if pain appears.
