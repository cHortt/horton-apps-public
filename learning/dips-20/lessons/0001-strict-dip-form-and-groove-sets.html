Strict dip form and groove sets

Mission: 20 strict dips, non-stop. Today: 7.

Today's win: after your next pull-up groove set, do 3 strict dips with the form below. That's the whole plan in miniature.

What counts as a strict dip

  1. Top: arms straight and locked, shoulders stacked above your hands, body straight.[1]
  2. Down: lower under control (about 2 seconds) until you feel a slight stretch in your shoulders, roughly upper arms parallel to the floor.[1] Pick that depth and hit the same one every rep.
  3. Up: press back to straight arms. No kicking, no bouncing out of the bottom.
  4. Torso: stay fairly upright. A big forward lean turns it into a chest dip, a different variation.[2]

Depth is a judgement call: deeper isn't better if it hurts. Controlled and consistent beats deep.

Rules for a groove set

  • Practise often and fresh, never to failure: the core of greasing the groove.[3]
  • Stop while every rep still moves at the same speed. Never grind.
  • Any elbow or shoulder pain: stop, take 2 days off, then return with smaller sets and shallower depth.
  • Missed a day? Don't make it up.
