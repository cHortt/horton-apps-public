Glossary

The words this course uses.

Strict dip: One rep on parallel bars from locked arms, down under control to a consistent depth, and back to lockout, with no kick or bounce.

Lockout: Arms fully straight at the top, shoulders over hands.

Depth: How low you go; in this course, a slight shoulder stretch, about upper arms parallel to the floor, the same every rep.

Max: The most strict dips in one unbroken set when fresh. Current max: 7.

Grease the groove (GTG): Frequent sub-maximal sets through the day, always stopping well before failure.

Groove set: One GTG set of about half your max, done right after a pull-up groove set.

Test: A single max set done fresh after a short warm-up.
