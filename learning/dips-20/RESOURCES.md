# 20 Dips Resources

## Knowledge

- [ExRx.net: Triceps Dip](https://exrx.net/WeightExercises/Triceps/BWTriDip)
  **Primary form source.** Shoulder-width bars, arms straight with shoulders above hands, lower until a slight stretch in the shoulders, press to straight arms. Use for: rep standard, depth.
- [ExRx.net: Chest Dip](https://exrx.net/WeightExercises/PectoralSternal/BWChestDip)
  The forward-lean variation. Use for: understanding torso angle; our standard stays the upright triceps dip.
- [Article: "Grease the Groove for Strength" by Pavel Tsatsouline (reprint)](https://humanmachine.wordpress.com/2007/06/22/grease-the-groove-for-strength/)
  Origin of grease-the-groove (GTG): frequent, sub-maximal, never-to-failure practice. Use for: GTG rules (stay fresh, avoid failure).
- [StrongFirst: Grease-the-Groove article archive](https://www.strongfirst.com/tag/grease-the-groove/)
  Further GTG reading on frequency and staying fresh.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/bodyweightfitness](https://www.reddit.com/r/bodyweightfitness/)
  Large bodyweight community. Use for: form checks (post a video), program critique.
