# Mission: 20 strict parallel-bar dips, non-stop

## Why
Personal goal: Collin wants 20 strict dips in one unbroken set, trained alongside his 20 pull-ups goal using the same short-session approach.

## Success looks like
- 20 strict reps on parallel bars: full lockout at the top, controlled descent to a consistent depth, no kicking or bouncing, no rest
- Dips and pull-ups practised together in quick sessions that fit ordinary days at home
- Knows how to adjust the plan himself when he stalls

## Constraints
- Equipment: dip bars on a home pull-up tower
- Time: many short sessions (minutes, not hours)
- No deadline; consistency over speed
- Joints first: any elbow or shoulder pain means stop and back off

## Out of scope
- Weighted dips, ring dips, bench dips, physique goals
